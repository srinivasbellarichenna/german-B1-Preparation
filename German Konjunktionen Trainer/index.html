Coordinating Conjunctions (Nebenordnende Konjunktionen)

Connect two main clauses (Hauptsätze). Verb position stays in 2nd place.

Structure:
Hauptsatz + Conjunction + Hauptsatz

Subordinating Conjunctions (Unterordnende Konjunktionen)

Introduce a Nebensatz and send the verb to the end.

Structure:
Hauptsatz + Conjunction + Nebensatz [verb at end]

Two-Part Conjunctions (Zweiteilige Konjunktionen)

Use two parts in different places in a sentence.

Structure:
Part 1... Hauptsatz ...Part 2 Hauptsatz

Conjunctive Adverbs (Konjunktionaladverbien)

Connect sentences; verb is always at 2nd position.

Structure:
Sentence 1. Adverb Sentence 2 [V in 2nd]

Memory Tips & Common Mistakes

⚠️ Common Mistake: Word Order

Wrong: Ich gehe nicht, weil ich bin müde.

Right: Ich gehe nicht, weil ich müde bin.

Subordinating conjunctions send the verb to the END!

💡 Memory Trick: WOBS

Weil, Obwohl, Bis, Seit = Verb at end

Remember these common subordinating conjunctions with WOBS!

🔍 Exam Tip: wenn vs. als

wenn: repeated/present/future actions

als: one-time past events only

This distinction appears frequently in B1 exams!
